Use **Albums** in the sidebar to organize photos by theme.

## Local Albums

### Create and search

1. Open **Albums** from the sidebar.
2. Click **Create album**.
3. Enter a name and click **Create**.

Enter an album name in the search field and click **Search** to filter the list. Select an album to view its description, photo count, and photos on the right.

### Add and reorder photos

- Use **Add from Gallery** in an album to open Gallery selection mode, then select photos to add.
- You can also use **Add to Album** in Gallery bulk selection mode. An album can contain up to 200 photos.
- Click **Edit** to change the description, remove photos from the album, or drag photos to reorder them.
- While editing, use **Discard changes** to restore the draft or **Done** to save it.

Removing a photo from an album does not delete the original file. Click a photo to open its detail sidebar.

### Delete an album

Choose **Delete album from this device**. This removes the album entry but does not delete the photo files.
