# Photographer Mode Guide

---

Photographer Mode is a full-screen view for checking newly captured photos. Open it from the VR icon in the title bar.

The main display controls are currently shown in Japanese in the app: `比較` (Compare), `選択を残す` (Keep selected), and `さらに10件` (Load 10 more).

## Start and exit

1. Click the **Photographer Mode** VR icon on the right side of the title bar.
2. The app enters full screen and shows recently imported photos in the filmstrip at the bottom.

Click **×** in the upper-right corner or press `Esc` to exit. The normal window view returns after closing the mode.

## Review photos

- Click a filmstrip thumbnail to switch photos.
- When at least two photos are available, use the left and right arrows to move between them.
- Use `−`, `＋`, and reset to change the zoom. You can also zoom with the mouse wheel and pan by dragging the photo.
- Click **さらに10件** (Load 10 more) to load older photos.

## Compare photos

1. Click **比較** (Compare).
2. Select photos from the candidate filmstrip (up to four).
3. Remove individual candidates and inspect them with the wheel and drag gestures.

Use **選択を残す** (Keep selected) to confirm moving photos left out of the comparison to the trash. Photos moved there can be restored from **Gallery > Trash**.

## Automatically show new photos

To automatically show new photos in VSA's VR capture mode, open **Settings > VR Mode > Open new photos in VR preview**. The panel appears after enabling Developer Mode in Settings > System. Adjust the preview curvature in the same panel. This trigger is separate from manually starting Photographer Mode from the title bar.

## Startup guidance

The current UI has no separate Photographer Mode setting for showing the startup guidance again. Use this guide when you need a refresher.
